Free OSRS NH and tribrid PvP practice

NH Trainer is a free, open-source browser practice client for Old School RuneScape-style PvP. Fight a trained AI opponent using NH stake or DMM setups, and practise gear switches, protection prayers, movement and supply timing without risking items or using a player account.

How to start practising

  1. Choose NH stake or DMM in the trainer above. Each setup loads its own Hard AI opponent.
  2. Arrange your inventory and equipment before the fight. Use Save setup to keep your setup in this browser.
  3. Wait for the opponent to load, then press Start. Focus on one skill at a time: clean switches, prayer changes or staying in control of your supplies.

What are NH and tribrid fights?

NH means “no honour”, a style of OSRS PvP that uses protection prayers and switches between melee, ranged and magic. Tribrid practice brings those three attack styles together: changing your gear, choosing an attack and defending against the opponent's next move.

Use repeated fights to work on switching accurately under pressure, responding with the right prayer, moving around freezes and finding time to eat or restore prayer.

Practise against a trained AI opponent

The opponent learns through self-play and chooses gear, prayers, attacks, movement and supplies from the fight state. The public trainer offers Hard difficulty, with the selected NH stake or DMM setup deciding which opponent model is loaded.

Read the NH AI training blog for the development story and how the opponents were trained.

Do I need to download a client or log in?

No download or RuneScape login is needed. The trainer runs in a browser with JavaScript and WebGL enabled. Your settings and saved setup stay in this browser. A mouse and keyboard give you the intended practice controls.

This is a standalone practice simulation. It does not connect to the live game, use player accounts or transfer items or progress into OSRS.
